07Our story

IT STARTED IN 1982.

The business began in 1982 as The Magazine Shop, serving the local community through newspapers and magazines. As customers’ needs changed, the business changed with them.

Today, Creative Craft by TMS carries that forward through stationery, customized printing and personalized products — and shopistry.ludhiana.shopping brings it online.

“The business evolved. The trust stayed.”
